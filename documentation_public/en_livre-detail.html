Book page

Why it exists: Everything your club knows about a book: cover, metadata, summary, average rating, rating breakdown, your own history, and other members' reviews with their comments. Example: Le Rêve du jaguar (Miguel Bonnefoy), Prix Femina and Grand Prix du roman de l'Académie française 2024.

Walkthrough

The cover is at the top left, with the metadata next to it (authors, year, ISBN, pages, prizes won). On the right, the 'Average rating' box shows the score out of 10, the number of voters, and a histogram of the distribution from 1 to 5 stars. Further down is the summary, then the action row: 'Rate / write a review', favorite (heart), add to a list (folder), and suggest (triangle).

The 'My review' section is empty until you rate the book; after that, it shows your rating and text. 'Other members' reviews' lists, for each review, the stars, the date, an excerpt, three reactions (thumbs up / question mark / thumbs down), and a 'Reply' link to post a comment under the review.
