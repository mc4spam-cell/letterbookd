Log in

Why it exists: Get into your club. letterbookd is private: access is by invitation link. If someone sent you a link, follow it; otherwise, use this page to resume your session.

Walkthrough

You land here if you are not logged in, or when you click 'Log in' in the top right. Enter the email address of your account and your password, then click 'Log in'. Tick 'Remember me' to stay logged in on this browser.

The 'Forgot password?' link sends a reset email. Follow it, choose a new password, then log in again. After logging in, you land on the home page.
